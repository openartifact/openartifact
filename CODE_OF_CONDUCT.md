# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
trolling, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in
issues, pull requests, reviews or any other project space. Maintainers may edit
or remove contributions, and restrict participation, to enforce this.

Report violations privately to **morf3089@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed.
